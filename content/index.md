### Wiki Rules & How to Contribute
1. **Don't delete** someone else's page—add a `> [!warning] Citation Needed` callout instead.
2. Wrap names, places, and jokes in `[[double brackets]]` to link pages together.
3. Drop screenshots and memes into the `Image Vault` folder.
